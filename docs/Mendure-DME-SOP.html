1. Purpose and Scope

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) trains staff on how to use the Mendure DME desktop application to manage durable medical equipment (DME) checkouts, returns, customer records, and loan agreements for the NW Montana Veterans Stand Down and Food Pantry DME Loan Program.

It covers day-to-day operations performed by staff and administrators. It does not cover software installation, network setup, or developer tasks.

2. System Overview

  • Application: Mendure DME, an offline desktop program (Windows). No internet connection is required to operate it.
  • Data storage: A local SQLite database stored per user at %APPDATA%\Mendure DME\database.db. Data never leaves the machine.
  • Checkout period: Equipment is loaned for 120 days. Due dates are calculated automatically and account for weekends and 11 federal holidays.
  • Agreements: Each checkout requires the customer to agree to the General Terms of Use and Loan Agreement and may include a digital signature.
  • Roles: The first account created is an Administrator. Additional users may be standard staff with limited permissions.
All user input is validated. Equipment IDs, phone numbers, ZIP codes, and dates must follow the formats described in Section 11.

3. Getting Started

3.1 First Run (Administrator Account)

  1. Launch the application. On the first run you will see Create Admin Account.
  2. Enter a username (at least 3 characters) and a password (at least 8 characters).
  3. Confirm the password and click Create Account. You are logged in automatically.
The first registered user becomes the Administrator. Choose a strong password and keep it secure.

3.2 Login

  1. Enter your username and password on the DME Checkout Login screen.
  2. Click Log In. On success you are taken to the Home page.
  3. After 5 failed login attempts within 5 minutes, login is temporarily locked.

3.3 Logout and Password Change

  • Click Logout in the top navigation bar at any time.
  • To change your password, open Settings, enter your current and new password (minimum 8 characters), then click Update Password. You will be logged out and must log in again.

5. Home (Master) Page

The Home page is the central workspace. It contains forms to add customers and equipment, a checkout panel, search and filters, tabs, and the equipment table.

5.1 Add a Customer

  1. In the Add Customer section, enter Name, Phone, and ZIP Code.
  2. Phone must contain at least 10 digits. ZIP must be exactly 5 digits.
  3. Click Add Customer. A success message confirms creation.

5.2 Add Equipment

  1. In the Add Equipment section, enter an Equipment ID in AA-0000 format (two letters, dash, four digits) and an Item Name.
  2. Click Add Equipment.

5.3 Check Out Equipment

  1. In the equipment table, check the Select box next to one or more available items.
  2. In the Checkout section, enter a customer reference (customer ID, name, phone, or ZIP).
  3. Optionally adjust the Checkout Date (defaults to today).
  4. Click Checkout Selected.
  • If multiple customers match the reference, a Confirm Customer Selection list appears. Click Select for the correct customer.
  • Loan records are created and the customer agreement is presented for signature (see Section 10).
  • If tablet signing is enabled and a second display is connected, the agreement is sent to that display.

5.4 Return Equipment

For a checked-out item, click Return in the Action column. A confirmation message appears and the item becomes available.

5.5 Extend a Loan

Click Extend on a checked-out item to extend the return date by the standard checkout period (120 days from the new date).

5.6 View an Agreement

Click View Agreement on a checked-out item (or from the Customers page) to display the stored agreement, equipment list, signature, and witness.

5.7 Filter, Search, and Sort

  • Search box: filters equipment, item name, customer, or phone.
  • Tabs: All, Checked Out, Available, Overdue (with counts).
  • Date filters: Checked Out From / To narrow by checkout date.
  • Column headers: click to sort ascending/descending by that column.

5.8 Inline Editing

Many table cells are editable directly: click a cell, type a new value, and press Enter or click away to save. Phone numbers are auto-formatted as (XXX) XXX-XXXX. Affected fields include Equipment ID, Item Name, Customer Name, Phone, Checked Out Date, and Due Date.

If an edit fails validation, the cell reverts to its original value and an error message is shown.

6. Customers Page

  • Search: filter by name, phone, or ZIP.
  • Add New Customer: form with name, phone, and ZIP.
  • Inline edit: name, phone, and ZIP columns are editable.
  • View Agreement: button appears if the customer has a stored agreement.
  • Delete: available to Administrators only, with confirmation.
  • Sort: by ID, Name, Phone, ZIP, or Date Added.

7. Equipment Page

  • Search: filter by equipment ID, item, or customer.
  • Add New Equipment: ID in AA-0000 format and item name.
  • Inline edit: Equipment ID, Item Name, and Date Verified.
  • Status column: shows Available or "Checked out to ... until ...".
  • Sell (Admin): for available items, opens a price dialog; records the sale and removes the item from inventory.
  • Delete (Admin): removes the equipment record (with confirmation).

9.4 Customer Signing (Tablet)

When a second display is connected (for example a tablet running Spacedesk), the customer agreement can be shown there so the customer signs on the tablet. If no second display is connected, agreements are signed on the main screen.

  • Toggle: "Send agreements to the second display" (Administrators only).
  • Status text shows whether a second display is detected.
  • The setting is persisted.

9.5 Import/Export Instructions

10. Customer Signing and Agreements

10.1 Inline Signing (Default)

  1. After checkout, the Customer Agreement screen opens.
  2. Review customer and equipment details; the checkout and return dates can be adjusted.
  3. The customer must check I agree to the General Terms of Use and Loan Agreement.
  4. Optionally capture a signature on the signature pad (Clear to redo).
  5. Optionally enter a Witnessed By name.
  6. Click Save Agreement to finalize. Equipment is now checked out.

Cancel removes the pending checkout and returns the items to available status.

10.2 Second-Display (Tablet) Signing

  • When enabled and a second display is detected, checkout sends the agreement to that display.
  • The Home page shows a Waiting for Customer Signature panel with a witness field.
  • Options on the Home panel: Sign on this screen (hands back to inline signing) or Cancel Checkout.
  • When the customer submits on the tablet, a success message appears and the list refreshes.

10.3 Interruptions

If signing is interrupted (window closed, display unplugged, or crash), the pending checkout is cancelled automatically so equipment cannot be left invisibly locked. A "Signing ended before the agreement was signed" message is shown.

10.4 Viewing a Stored Agreement

Open View Agreement to see customer details, agreement date, equipment and return-by dates, the full terms, the signature image, and witness. Use Print to print or save as PDF.

10.5 General Terms (Summary)

The agreement states that the equipment is loaned under the NW MT Veterans Stand Down and Food Pantry DME Loan Program; the recipient accepts responsibility for the equipment and its safe use; use is at the recipient's own risk; and therapists are also responsible for returning or paying for equipment by the due date. The full text is displayed in the application during signing.

12. Data Management and Backup

  • Location: %APPDATA%\Mendure DME\database.db (plus -wal / -shm sidecar files).
  • Backup: use Settings → Export to save Customers, Equipment, Checkout Log, and Equipment List as Excel or CSV. Store backups in a safe location.
  • Import: use Settings → Import to load customers or equipment from Excel/CSV.
  • First-run migration: if a legacy database is found next to the application, it is copied into the user data folder on first run. Existing data is never overwritten.
  • Shutdown: Administrators can shut down the application from Settings.
Because data is local and unencrypted, protect the machine and back up regularly.
